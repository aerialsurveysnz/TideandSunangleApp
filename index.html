Tide heights between LINZ high and low waters use cosine interpolation, the method LINZ describes for estimating intermediate heights. Predictions are for the port itself and do not include weather effects such as storm surge or wind set-up. Check your survey site's own tidal offsets if it is away from the port.

Sun elevation includes standard atmospheric refraction (under 0.1° above 20°). Bundled LINZ data covers 1 Oct 2026 – 30 Jun 2027 for 13 standard ports; add LINZ CSVs for other ports or later dates.
